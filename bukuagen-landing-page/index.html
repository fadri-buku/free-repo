Questions people actually ask.

Is it legal to carry?

In the US, EU, and most of Asia — yes. The K1 has no locking blade and no sharpened edge over an inch, so it qualifies as a tool rather than a knife. Check your local rules; we link to a country-by-country guide in your order email.

Will it set off airport security?

It hasn't yet, in 3 years of carry-on testing across 40+ airports. It's metal, so it will show on the scan — but the geometry is intentionally non-bladed, so it's been waved through by TSA, EU, and APAC security. We can't promise every screener will agree, though.

When does it ship?

The first pre-order batch ships May 2026. After that we ship within 7 business days of order.

What if I don't like it?

Carry it for 60 days. If it doesn't earn its place in your pocket, send it back for a full refund — even engraved units.

Why titanium and not steel?

Titanium is roughly 40% lighter than steel at comparable strength, doesn't rust, doesn't trigger nickel allergies, and wears in beautifully rather than rusting out. It's also why this thing costs $129 and not $39.
